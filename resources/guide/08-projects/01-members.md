# Members and roles

A project's people are in **Project settings**, under **Members**. Add someone by their email address: they need an account here already.

| Role   | Can                                                       |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner  | Everything: rename the project, manage members, delete it |
| Editor | Add and change what is in it, and its connections         |
| Viewer | Look, and download from its Drive                         |

Change someone's role, or remove them, from the same list.

## What belongs to the project

Its notes, boards, tables, trips, Drive, workflows and connections belong to the project, not to whoever made them. Someone who leaves takes nothing with them, and nothing is lost.

## Its dashboard

A project's **Dashboard** shows what people changed lately, the to-dos still open in its notes, its chat, and its members.

## Leave or delete

**Leave project** takes you out; what you made stays. **Delete project** deletes everything in it, for everyone, and cannot be undone.
