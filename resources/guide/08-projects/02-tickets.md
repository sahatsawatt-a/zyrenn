# Tickets

**Tickets** keeps what a project has been asked to do: a **bug** to fix, or a **feature** to add. Each has a number in its project, such as #12.

![A project's tickets as a board of columns](/images/guide/tickets.png)

## File one

**New ticket** opens a short form:

- **Bug or feature.** For a bug: what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. For a feature: what it should do, and for whom.
- **Title**, in a line.
- **Part**: which part of the work it is about, in the project's own words, such as "Boards".
- **Priority**: low, normal or high.
- **Given to**: someone in the project, or nobody yet.
- **Pictures**: a screenshot says more than words; add them from this computer or the Drive.

## Follow it

Move a ticket along as it is worked on: **Open**, **In progress**, **Done**, or **Won't do**. See them **As a list**, or **As a board** of a column for each, and drag a card to the next column.

Narrow the tickets to bugs or features, or to one part, and search them. The counts beside All, Bugs and Features are of those still to do.

## Talk it over

**Discuss**, on a ticket, starts a chat about it that everyone in the project can join. Once it has one, the button opens it.

:::callout 💡
An AI client connected over MCP can read and work through a project's tickets too: list them, take one, and mark it in progress and then done.
:::
