# See a table on a map

A table with a **Location** field can be seen as a map. Choose **Map** from **View**.

- Each row with a place is a pin.
- **Colour by** colours the pins by a field, such as a Select field for the kind of place.
- Click a pin for its row, and **Show in the grid** to go to it.

A place in a Location field can be one of your saved places: then it shows the place as you saved it, and follows when you change it in Maps.

:::callout 💡
For a plan of where to go, day by day and in order, use a trip. See Maps and trips.
:::
