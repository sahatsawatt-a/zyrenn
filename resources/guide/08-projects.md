# Projects and tickets

A project is a shared space. Its notes, boards, tables, trips, Drive and workflows belong to the project, not to whoever made them, so they stay when someone leaves.

<video src="/images/guide/tour-projects.webm" title="Show me: Switch to a project"></video>

## In short

- Open the switcher at the top of the sidebar and choose **New project**. The same switcher moves you between your own space and each project you are in.
- Add people in **Project settings**, each an **owner**, an **editor** or a **viewer**.
- Everyone with a note or a board open works on the same one, and sees the others' changes as they happen.
- **Tickets** keeps the bugs to fix and the features to add, from open to done.
- A project has its own Drive, its own chat groups, and its own connections, so nobody's own key pays for shared work.
