# Settings and AI clients

Open your name at the foot of the sidebar and choose **Settings**.

<video src="/images/guide/tour-settings.webm" title="Show me: Connect an AI client"></video>

| Page        | What is there                                |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Profile     | Your name and email address                  |
| Security    | Your password, two-step sign-in and passkeys |
| Appearance  | Light, dark, or whatever your device uses    |
| MCP         | Tokens for AI clients to reach your content  |
| Connections | The models, bots and keys your workflows use |
