# Saved places

## Find a place

In **Maps**, type in **Search places**, or click anywhere on the map. The place's card opens with its name and address, its coordinates to copy, and a link to it in OpenStreetMap.

**Opening hours & rating** asks Google for the place's hours, its stars and its website.

## Keep it

Save a place to a list of your own: "Want to go", "Clients", "Coffee". **New list** starts one. Each list has its own colour and icon, and can be shown or hidden on the map with its eye.

A saved place keeps a **Note**: the opening hours, who to ask for, the side entrance. Rename it on its card.

## Used everywhere, kept in one place

A saved place can be a stop on a trip, a value in a table's **Location** field, or a map in a note. Wherever it is used, it shows the place as you saved it, and follows when you change it.

## Lists in and out

**Export GeoJSON** saves your places as a file other map apps can read; **Import GeoJSON** brings places in from one.

## Map styles

The buttons at the top of the map change its look: **Map**, **Light**, **Dark** or **Satellite**.
