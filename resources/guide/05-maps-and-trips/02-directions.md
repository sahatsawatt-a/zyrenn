# Directions

**Directions**, on a place's card, finds the way there. Choose where to start, or **Start here** on another place's card; either end can be typed, or clicked on the map. Swap them with the arrows between.

The way is drawn on the map, with how long it takes and how far it is, walking, driving or cycling.

Times are worked out with Valhalla on OpenStreetMap. For buses and trains, open the way in Amap or Google Maps.
