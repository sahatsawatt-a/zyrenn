# AI clients over MCP

Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini, Codex, Windsurf, Zed, Cline and other AI clients can read and change your notes, boards, tables, trips, places, Drive, workflows and tickets, through MCP.

![MCP settings, with what to paste into a client](/images/guide/settings-mcp.png)

## Connect a client

1. Under **Settings**, then **MCP**, give a token a name, such as "Claude Code on laptop", and press **Create token**.
2. Copy it straight away: it is not shown again.
3. Under **Connect a client**, choose **HTTP** (from any device that reaches the app) or **stdio** (on this machine), then your client. What to paste or run is shown, with your new token in it.

**Active tokens** lists each token with when it was last used. **Revoke** one to cut off the client using it.

## What to ask

Ask in your own words; the client finds pages by their names:

- "Add 'rain jacket' to my Packing list note."
- "Draw our sign-up flow on a board called Sign-up, as a flowchart."
- "Make a table of these ten clients, with a Location column."
- "Plan two days in Kyoto as a trip, with the temples on day one."
- "Put this picture in my Drive and show it in the Weekly review note."
- "Go through the open tickets in the Website project, and mark each done as you fix it."

It reads a long note or a big board a part at a time, and changes only what it needs, so it is quick even on large pages.

## In a project

Name the project in what you ask, and the client works there, as you, with your role: a viewer's client can read but not change anything.

:::callout ⚠️
A token acts as you. Keep it like a password, and revoke one you no longer use.
:::
