# Pictures and videos

**More**, in the bar at the foot of the board, adds:

- **A picture**: from this computer, your Drive, a link, or SVG markup pasted in.
- **A video**: from this computer, your Drive, or a link. It plays on the board, with its own controls.

A picture can also be pasted or dropped onto the board. What is uploaded is kept in the Drive.

Select a picture to resize it, or to open **Edit photo**: crop, turn, straighten, change its light and colour, or hide part of it. The original stays in the Drive.
