# Frames, presenting and export

A frame (**F**) is a 16:9 area on the board, like a slide. Whatever is inside it moves with it, so a board can be laid out as a deck.

## Present

**Present** plays the board's frames one screen at a time, full screen, in order. **Esc** ends it.

## Export

**Export** shows a preview first, then saves:

- **PDF**: one file, with a page for each frame.
- **Pictures**: a PNG of each frame.

A board with no frames comes out whole, on one page or in one picture.

A frame's own menu downloads just that frame as a picture, copies it, or saves it to your Drive.

## Leave a frame out

In **Layers**, a frame can be hidden from the PDF while it stays on the board, for working notes beside the slides.

:::callout 💡
A note can show one frame of a board: type **/board** in the note and choose the frame. It stays up to date as the board changes.
:::
