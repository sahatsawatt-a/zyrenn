# Shapes, sticky notes and text

The bar along the foot of the board holds what can go on it. Pick a tool, then press and drag on the board where it should go.

| Tool        | Key | Puts down                                     |
| ----------- | --- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Select      | V   | Nothing: chooses and moves what is there      |
| Sticky note | S   | A square note, for an idea or a comment       |
| Text        | T   | Words with no box round them                  |
| Rectangle   | R   | A box, for a step                             |
| Ellipse     | O   | A circle or an oval                           |
| Decision    | M   | A diamond, for a question with ways out of it |
| Connector   | A   | A line from one thing to another              |
| Pen         | D   | A line drawn by hand                          |
| Frame       | F   | A 16:9 area, like a slide                     |
| Formula     | E   | A formula written in LaTeX                    |

**Esc** goes back to Select.

## Every shape

**Shapes** opens all of them, with a search: rectangles, pills, ellipses, triangles, diamonds, hexagons and stars; the flowchart set (database, data, document, process, cloud, manual input, delay, display and more); and arrows, speech bubbles, a heart, a cube, a brace.

Three sets stay folded until they are opened, and then stay open for you:

- **Software**: tables, classes, actors, packages, components, notes and lifelines. See Diagrams.
- **Architecture**: users, browsers, phones, computers, servers, queues, buckets, gateways, firewalls and the internet.
- **Electrical**: parts with pins, joined by wires. See Circuits.

## Write on a shape

**Double-click** a shape or a sticky note to write on it, and press **Enter** when you are done. A shape's words can be a small piece of Markdown: `# ` for a heading, `- ` for a list, `**bold**` and `*italic*`.

## Change how it looks

Select something, and the panel on the right shows its **Fill** and **Line** colours, its font, its text size, the room between its edge and its words, and its order: bring it forward, send it back. **Turn a quarter turn** (**R**) turns it.

With nothing selected, **Restyle the board** gives everything on it one look at once.

## Choose and move

Click to select, **Shift + click** to add to what is selected, or drag across the bare board to select all that the drag covers. **Ctrl + D** duplicates; **Delete** removes. Guides show when what you move lines up with what is beside it.
