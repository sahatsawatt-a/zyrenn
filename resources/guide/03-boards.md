# Boards

A board is an endless whiteboard. Put shapes, sticky notes, words, pictures and arrows anywhere on it, and group them into frames you can present or export.

<video src="/images/guide/tour-shapes.webm" title="Show me: Join two shapes"></video>

![A board with a few steps joined by connectors](/images/guide/boards.png)

## In short

- The bar along the foot of the board holds what can go on it. Pick a tool, then press and drag where it should go. **Double-click** anything to write on it.
- Connectors join one thing to another, and stay joined as things move.
- The panel on the right changes whatever is selected: its colours, its words, its order.
- **Templates**, at the top, starts a flowchart, an org chart, a data model and more.
- **Present** plays the frames as slides; **Export** saves a PDF or pictures.
- In a project, everyone with the board open works on the same one, and sees the others' changes as they are made. There is no Save button.
