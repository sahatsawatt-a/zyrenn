# Drive

The Drive keeps your files: pictures, PDFs, documents, videos, anything. Notes, boards, chats and workflows take their files from it, so a file is uploaded once and used anywhere.

<video src="/images/guide/tour-drive.webm" title="Show me: The Drive"></video>

## In short

- **Upload**, or drop files on the page. **New folder** makes a folder; drag a file onto one to move it.
- Sort and narrow the files by kind; search them by name.
- A picture opens full size, a video plays, a PDF can be read.
- **Edit photo** crops, turns, straightens, hides part of a picture, and changes its light and colour, keeping the original.
- A project has a Drive of its own, for everyone in it.
