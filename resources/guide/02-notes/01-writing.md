# Text, headings and lists

Most of a note is words. Write as you would anywhere: the note keeps itself as you go, and there is no Save button.

## Headings

**/heading 1**, **/heading 2** and **/heading 3** give three sizes. In Markdown, and as a shortcut while typing, a heading is a line that starts with `#`, `##` or `###` and a space.

## Lists

| Type, or start a line with | To get                    |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| /bullet list, or `- `      | A list with dots          |
| /numbered list, or `1. `   | A list that counts itself |
| /to-do list, or `[] `      | A list with boxes to tick |

**Tab** moves an item in under the one above it, and **Shift + Tab** moves it back out. **Enter** on an empty item ends the list.

- [x] Book the flights
- [ ] Pack a charger
- [ ] Ask Sam about the date

The to-dos still open in your notes are on your **Dashboard**.

## Words that stand out

| Type, or press        | To get             |
| --------------------- | ------------------ |
| `**words**`, Ctrl + B | **bold**           |
| `*words*`, Ctrl + I   | _italic_           |
| `~~words~~`           | ~~struck through~~ |
| `` `words` ``         | `code`             |

Paste a web address to make a link, or paste it over words you have selected to link them. Click a link for its menu: edit it, copy it, or show it as a card with the page's title and picture.

## Quotes and callouts

**/quote** sets words apart as something said:

> Make it work, then make it right, then make it fast.

**/callout** puts them in a coloured box with a sign at its start, for a tip, a warning or a decision:

:::callout ⚠️
The venue closes at 22:00. Everything has to be out by then.
:::

## Move, turn and copy blocks

Hover a block for the handle to its left. Drag the handle to move the block; click it for the block's menu, to turn it into another kind, duplicate it, or delete it.
