# Tables in a note

**/table** puts a small table in the note: rows and columns with a header row, for a comparison or a short list of facts.

| Plan  | Price | Seats |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Solo  | Free  | 1     |
| Team  | $8    | 10    |
| Large | $20   | 50    |

Click into a cell to write in it, and **Tab** to go to the next. While you are in the table, its bar inserts a row above or below, a column left or right, deletes a row or a column, turns the header row on or off, and merges or splits cells.

:::callout 💡
For data that grows, sorts, filters or works out sums, use a **table** of its own from Tables. A note can still show a number from it with **/value**.
:::
