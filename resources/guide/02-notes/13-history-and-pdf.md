# History, PDF and writing together

## Writing together

In a project, everyone with a note open writes in the same copy. You see the others' cursors as they type, and who has it open, in the bar above the note.

## Version history

**Version history**, in the bar above the note, lists earlier versions of it, kept as it is written. **Restore** brings one back; the note as it was is kept first, so a restore can be undone too.

**Pin** keeps the note as it is now as a version that stays, with a label such as "Sent to the client". Pinned versions are listed first.

## Export a PDF

**Export PDF** shows the PDF before you save it, in one of two looks:

- **Simple**: as the note looks on screen.
- **Report**: serif type, with a header and page numbers.

A block's menu, from its handle, can leave that block out of the PDF, and **/page break** starts a new page where you want one.

## The rest

The **⋯** menu holds the note's width, its links, the folder it is in, and deleting it.
