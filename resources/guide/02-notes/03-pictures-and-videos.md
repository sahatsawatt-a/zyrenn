# Pictures and videos

**/image** puts a picture in the note, and **/video** a video that plays in place. Each comes from this computer, from your Drive, or from a link. A picture can also be pasted or dropped straight into the note.

Whatever is uploaded is kept in the Drive, so the same picture can be used again elsewhere.

## Sizes

Hover a picture or a video for its bar: make it small, medium or the full width of the note.

## Full size

Click a picture to see it full size, with the note's other pictures and videos a swipe away. A video playing in the note carries on from the same moment.

## Edit a photo

**Edit photo**, on a picture's bar, crops it, turns it, straightens it, changes its light and colour, or hides part of it: a face, a number plate or a password, blurred, pixelated or blacked out. The original stays in the Drive. The photo editor is the same as the Drive's.

:::callout 💡
A PDF cannot play a video, so a note's PDF says which video was there instead.
:::
