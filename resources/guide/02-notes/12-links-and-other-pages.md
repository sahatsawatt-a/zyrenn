# Links and other pages

A note can point at your other pages, and show them inside it, kept up to date.

## Link to a page

Type **[[** and the first letters of a page's name, then pick it from the list. The link shows the page's name, and still finds it after it is renamed. It can point at a note, a board, a table or a trip.

In Markdown, a link to a page is its name between double brackets: `[[Packing list]]`, or `[[Packing list|what to bring]]` for other words, or `[[board:Launch plan]]` for a page that is not a note.

At its foot, a note lists the notes that link to it, under **Linked from**.

## Show another page in the note

| Type   | To show                                                          |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| /board | A board, or one of its frames, as it is now                      |
| /trip  | A whole trip, or one day of it with its stops and the way        |
| /map   | A place on a map, or one of your saved places                    |
| /link  | A web page as a card, with its title and picture                 |
| /value | A number from a table or a trip, in a sentence (see Live values) |

What is shown follows the page it comes from: change the board, and the note shows the change.

## Links to the web

Paste a web address and choose how it shows: as a link in the text, as a card, or, for a map address, as a map.
