# The photo editor

**Edit photo**, on a picture in the Drive, in a note or on a board, opens the photo editor. What you change is saved as a new file beside the original, which stays as it was, so you can always go back to it.

![The photo editor, with its tools beside the picture](/images/guide/drive-photo.png)

## Crop

Drag the corners, or choose a size: **Free**, the **Original**'s, **1:1**, **4:3**, **3:4**, **16:9** or **9:16**. Choose a shape too: a **Rectangle**, **Rounded** corners, or a **Circle**, for a face in a profile picture.

## Turn and straighten

**Turn & flip** turns it a quarter left or right, or flips it. **Straighten** turns it a degree at a time, for a horizon that leans.

## Hide part of it

Cover a face, a number plate or a password: draw a box over it and choose **Blur**, **Pixelate** or **Black box**. A black box hides text for certain; a blur may not.

## Looks, light and colour

**Looks** are ready-made styles to start from. **Light & colour** sets **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation**, **Warmth** and a **Vignette**, darker at the edges.

## Before you save

Press and hold **Hold to compare** (or hold **\\**) to see the original. **Undo** and **Redo** step through your changes, and **Reset to original** starts again. Choose the size to save at, then **Save**.
