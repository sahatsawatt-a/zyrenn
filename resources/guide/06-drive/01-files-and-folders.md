# Files and folders

## Put files in

Press **Upload**, or drop files anywhere on the page; dropped on a folder, they go into it. Pictures pasted or dropped into a note, a board or a chat come here too, so everything you use is in one place.

## Keep them in order

- **New folder** makes a folder, inside the one you are in.
- A file's or folder's menu can **rename** it, **move** it to another folder, or **delete** it.
- Drag a file onto a folder to move it there, or onto the folder above to move it up.

## Find them

**Search** looks through every folder by name. Sort by newest, oldest, name or size, and show only one kind: pictures, PDFs, documents, audio, video, archives or the rest.

## Open them

Click a picture to see it full size, a video to play it, and a PDF to read it. **Download** saves any file to your computer.

## A project's Drive

A project has a Drive of its own, which everyone in it can open. Its viewers can look and download, but not add or change anything.

:::callout ⚠️
Deleting is for good. A note that showed the file shows a broken picture instead.
:::
