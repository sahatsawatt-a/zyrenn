# Chat rooms

A chat room of your own is a place that you and your workflows write in. Press **New Chat Room** in **Chat**, and give it a title.

## Write in it

**Enter** sends; **Shift + Enter** starts a new line. A file can be pasted or dropped in, or attached from this device, from the Drive, or a place from the map. Pictures and videos show in the chat; other files as a link.

## An AI that answers

A model answers in a room through a workflow:

1. A **Chat message** start, set to this room.
2. **Ask AI**, with your model's connection, asking it `{{ trigger.text }}`.
3. **Write in a room**, writing the answer back.

The templates **AI answers on Telegram** and **AI answers on LINE** are the same, for a bot or a channel.

## A chat from outside, kept here

A room can hold a Telegram or LINE chat, both ways: what people send there shows here, and what you write here goes to them. The templates **Telegram chats in a room, both ways** and **LINE chats in a room, both ways** set it up. **About this chat** then shows who it is with, the service, and notes of your own about them.
