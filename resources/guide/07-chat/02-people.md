# Messages with people

## One to one

**New message** writes to anyone you share a project with. The conversation is listed under **Direct messages**, and kept for both of you.

## Groups

**Groups** belong to a project. Start one for a topic from the project's Chat with **New group**: give it a name and choose who is in it. Anyone else in the project can **Join** it later, and anyone can **Leave**.

A ticket's discussion is a group too: **Discuss**, on a ticket, starts it or opens it.

## What you can send

Words, emoji (an emoji sent on its own is drawn large), pictures, videos, files and places on the map. Each room shows how many messages you have not read, and the sidebar shows the total.

## About a chat

**About this chat** shows who is in it, and keeps notes of your own about them, such as a phone number or a birthday: **Add a note**, say what it is, and its value.
