# Maps and trips

**Maps** is for places you want to keep: a café, a client's office, a view. **Trips** plans travel day by day, with the flights, the hotels, and each day's stops in the order you will go.

<video src="/images/guide/tour-places.webm" title="Show me: Maps and trips"></video>

![A saved place's card on the map: directions, its list, opening hours and a note](/images/guide/maps.png)

## In short

- Search for a place, or click the map, then save it to a list of your own.
- A saved place keeps a note, and shows the same wherever it is used: in a trip, a table or a note.
- **Directions** finds the way from one place to another.
- A trip has its flights, its hotels, and each day's stops in order, with the way between them and what it all costs.
