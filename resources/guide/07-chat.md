# Chat

**Chat** holds your own chat rooms, and your messages with the people you work with.

<video src="/images/guide/tour-chat.webm" title="Show me: Find your chats"></video>

![A chat room, with a workflow's answer in it](/images/guide/chat.png)

## In short

- A **chat room** is yours, for you and your workflows to write in. A workflow can have an AI model answer there, or keep a Telegram or LINE chat in it, both ways.
- **Direct messages** are one to one, with anyone you share a project with.
- **Groups** belong to a project; anyone in it can join them.
- Send words, emoji, pictures, files and places. **Enter** sends.
